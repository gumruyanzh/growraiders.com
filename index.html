Grow. Build. Raid.

Run an independent grow in a sunny, legal-grow world, then take your Buds out to Raid.

  1. Grow

    Grow your Strains

    Every fighter in your Army is a Bud you grew yourself in a Grow Room. Each Strain has its own character and job: Indica Strains soak up hits, Sativa Strains hit fast, and Hybrid Strains keep the Army going.

    Collect Seeds to unlock new Strains and upgrade the ones you have. Each new Bloom adds a piece of gear, from Sprout to Prize Bloom.

    • Indica
    • Sativa
    • Hybrid

  2. Build

    Build your Grow Op

    Lay out your Plot your way. Producers make Cash and Nutrients, Storages keep them, Grow Rooms grow your Buds, and slapstick Defenses like the Guard-Dog Kennel and the Net Launcher keep raiders busy.

    Upgrade your Grow House to open new Buildings, and watch your Grow Op change from a Corner Lot to a Backyard Greenhouse to a Market Garden.

    • Cash
    • Nutrients

  3. Raid

    Raid rival growers

    Scout another player's Grow Op, deploy your Buds around its edge and see how much you can Destroy before the timer runs out. The more you Destroy, the bigger your share of their Cash and Nutrients, and the more Rep you win.

    You Raid a saved copy of their Grow Op, so they never need to be online, and our server checks every Raid, so nobody can fake a result. When you come back, the game shows each Raid on your own Grow Op while you were away. Kief can never be stolen.

    • Rep
    • Stealable Cash

  4. Missions

    Beat Big Weed's campaign

    Big Weed is a cartoon megacorporation that thinks independent Grow Ops are off-brand. Play handcrafted Missions against its Facilities, one World at a time, from the Seedling Depot to the Flagship Facility.

    Clear each World's Head Office to open its Seed Locker and free the Strain kept inside.

    Memo

    From Big Weed: Independent Grow Ops are off-brand. Please acquire them by Q3. Thanks in advance.

Fair play

  • Speed and style, never power

    No loot boxes: every item shows exactly what you get. Kief, the game's own currency, can never be stolen in a Raid, and Seeds are earned by playing, never sold.

  • Nothing random

    Every Shop offer has fixed contents, shown in full before you buy. Prices are the App Store's real prices, and an end date is always a real date.

  • No ads, no tracking

    No ads, no ad trackers and no tracking across other apps or websites. The privacy policy lists exactly what the game collects.
